# Scouting (2026-09-20)

Asked for by the user after rush-13 (Quicksilver, the lobby's boxes): "we need a scouting system: track when we last
see each mex spot, order single Pawns or Ticks to explore the map occasionally, and prioritise mapping the enemy's
base in the opening minutes. From first contact at 1:52 we have enough information for a high guess that the enemy's
mexes are all in the vicinity of D7, the reasonable start spot. C7/B7 should be low priority, the same as F6, but
still checked at some point."

## What exists and goes

- H-ARMY-SCOUT (`army.rs`): from minute 3 one raider every 90 s walked to the attack target and on to the enemy
  start, so the wave gate had seen something. Never measured. Deleted: the scout below replaces it.
- H-ARMY-PRESSURE's `visited` list of box spots (rush-7): the party's own memory of where it had stood. Deleted: the
  spot knowledge below is shared by every rule.
- H-MAP-ENEMY-START: a guessed base snaps to the walkable spot nearest the box centre. Kept, and refined below.

## Design

**Spot knowledge (H-SCOUT-SPOTS, `scout.rs`).** For every metal spot, the last frame it was within an own unit's
sight (the simulator's sight per unit type; buildings count). Surveyed every half second. A spot seen within 60 s is
fresh; one never seen counts as 600 s stale.

**The likely base (H-MAP-ENEMY-CLUSTER, `bases.rs`).** The first building of theirs seen within 2500 of an unfound
base's guess moves the guess to the metal-spot cluster nearest that building (spots chained within 700 of one
another), at the cluster's spot nearest its centroid: the reasonable start spot. The base stays "unfound" until a
factory is seen, as before. On Quicksilver the first contact at 1:52 is an extractor at (3392, 4912) and the cluster
is D7's.

**What is worth a look (`spots_to_look_at`).** From a point, spots ranked by staleness (seconds since seen) times
likelihood over the walk there (floored at 300 elmos): likelihood 3 within 1800 of a live enemy base (guessed, refined
or found), 1 inside an enemy start box, 0.3 elsewhere (C7/B7, F6: checked eventually, when nothing better is stale).
Fresh spots and unreachable spots are left out; a scout keeps a berth of 450 from any known armed building, the
pressure party prices those itself.

**The scout (H-SCOUT-ROUTE).** From 2:00, one raider from the home group (not the pressure party) at a time. It is
sent when no base of theirs is found, or the freshest spot around the presumed base is older than 60 s, or 90 s have
passed since the last route. Its route is four spots: the best, then greedily the nearest of the top eight from each
point on, as queued Move orders (not Fight: it looks, it does not stop to shoot). It is out of the home group while
on its route.

**Users of the knowledge.** H-ARMY-PRESSURE's target when nothing is known and its `harass_elsewhere` take the
spots of likelihood 1 or more, nearest first, in place of the box-spot list. Later: the LLM's briefing (spots by
staleness), H-ECO-EXPAND's choice of spots.
