The game: Beyond All Reason, a real-time strategy game. We win by destroying the enemy commander; we lose when ours dies. Metal is everything: extractors on metal spots are the income, income becomes soldiers, and the bigger army kills the smaller one and then the base behind it. A side doing well holds about 5 extractors by minute 4, 9 by minute 10 and 15 by minute 15. Extractors cost 50 metal and pay back in a minute; a lab costs 650; a light turret 85; a constructor 120. Energy only needs to keep the store from emptying: laser turrets stop firing and building slows in an energy stall. Metal in the bank is army we do not have.
The opponent (an AI): it raids from the first minutes with small squads of raiders that go for builders and extractors, the outermost and least defended first, and skip targets with a turret and a couple of soldiers beside them. It hoards its main army at home and sends it as one block when it judges itself stronger than what it has seen of ours. Its commander stays at its base and is the win condition; its base gets turrets over time.
Units: raiders (Pawn, Grunt) are fast and light: they kill builders, extractors and lone turrets under construction, and lose to line units. Line units (Mace, Thug) win most fights at equal metal and are slow. Rocket skirmishers outrange turrets. Fights are decided by the metal of soldiers on the spot, a turret counting three times its metal: never walk into a turret line at parity; arrive together. A group of ours advancing (fight_to) fights its way through everything, turrets and the commander included: that is how a base is attacked, and it dies if the odds are against it. A group holding or engaging stands against mobile units and steps out of turret reach on its own; a group ordered to move without fighting runs from everything.
The menu: every second each actor that is free, or has been on its task for ten seconds, is offered a choice of next actions. "continue" keeps its task; choose something else only when it is clearly better. Places are named: home, enemy_base, spot_N (a metal spot, numbered as in the player's map), passage_N (a narrow way between the two sides). Numbers in this picture are for reference; the words beside them say what matters.
