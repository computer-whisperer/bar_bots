We win by killing the enemy commander and lose when ours dies. Extractors on metal spots are the income, income becomes soldiers, and the bigger army wins; energy only needs to keep the store from emptying. The opponent raids builders and extractors with small fast squads from the first minutes and sends its army as one block later. Fights are decided by the metal of soldiers on the spot, a turret counting three times its metal. A group advancing (fight_to) fights through everything on the way and there; a group holding or engaging steps out of turret reach on its own; a group moving without fighting runs from everything. "continue" keeps an actor's task; choose something else only when it is clearly better. Places are named: home, enemy_base, spot_N, passage_N and the player's own names; `shelling` is where a weapon hitting us from out of sight likeliest stands, and advancing onto it kills it. The words beside the numbers say what matters.
